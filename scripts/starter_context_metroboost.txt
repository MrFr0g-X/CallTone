MetroBoost Telecom — Customer Service Policy & Knowledge Base

GREETING
Thank you for calling MetroBoost Telecom, my name is [agent name]. May I have your name, and how can I help you today?

CLOSING
Is there anything else I can help you with? Thank you for being a MetroBoost customer — have a great day.

IDENTITY VERIFICATION
Before discussing any account details, verify the customer's full name, the mobile number on the account, and the last 4 digits of the account PIN. Never read back the full PIN or password.

HOLD PROCEDURE
Ask permission before placing the customer on hold, give an estimated wait time, and check back at least every 60 seconds.

TRANSFER PROCEDURE
Explain why a transfer is needed, name the team you are transferring to, and stay on the line until the receiving agent answers.

ESCALATION PROCEDURE
Escalate to a supervisor when the customer requests it, when the issue is outside your authority, or after two failed resolution attempts.

MANDATORY DISCLOSURES
This call may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Plan changes take effect on the next billing cycle. Early termination fees may apply to contract plans.

PROHIBITED PHRASES
Do not say "calm down", "that's not my problem", or "there is nothing I can do". Never promise a refund or credit you are not authorized to approve.

PRODUCTS AND SERVICES
Prepaid and postpaid mobile plans, home fibre internet, MetroBoost TV bundles, international roaming packs, and device instalment plans.

CURRENT PROMOTIONS
Unlimited 5G data for the first 3 months on new postpaid plans. Free router with any 12-month fibre contract.

POLICIES
Billing disputes must be raised within 60 days of the invoice date. Approved credits appear within 1-2 billing cycles. Roaming must be enabled before travel.

COMMON TROUBLESHOOTING
For no signal: toggle airplane mode, restart the device, and check for a network outage in the area. For slow fibre: power-cycle the router and confirm no data cap has been reached.

CONTACT INFORMATION
Support: 16500 (24/7). Fault reporting: 16501. Email: support@metroboost.example.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Q: When does my plan renew? A: On your monthly billing date. Q: How do I enable roaming? A: In the MetroBoost app under Plan > Roaming, or by calling support.

TONE GUIDELINES
Warm, professional, and patient. Acknowledge the customer's concern before moving to a solution. Mirror their pace.

EMPATHY GUIDELINES
Acknowledge frustration explicitly ("I understand how frustrating that is") before explaining the next steps.

CONFLICT RESOLUTION GUIDELINES
Stay calm, do not interrupt, take ownership of the issue, and focus on what you can do for the customer.

RESOLUTION EXPECTATIONS
Resolve billing and connectivity issues within the call where possible. Always confirm the resolution and any timelines before closing.
