# Rate Politeness & Tone

## Purpose

Evaluates the agent's communication style, professionalism, and courtesy throughout the call.

## Weight

15% of overall score

## Scoring Rubric

- 100 = Consistently professional, warm, and courteous throughout
- 75 = Generally polite with minor lapses
- 50 = Mixed tone - sometimes professional, sometimes not
- 25 = Frequently unprofessional or discourteous
- 0 = Rude, dismissive, or hostile
